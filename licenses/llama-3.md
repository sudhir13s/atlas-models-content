## Plain-English
Meta Llama 3 may be used, copied, modified and redistributed worldwide at no charge, including
commercially. Distributing it, or a product that uses it, means giving the recipient the agreement,
showing "Built with Meta Llama 3" and keeping Meta's attribution notice. A model you train or improve
with the materials must have a name that starts with "Llama 3". Unlike the later 3.2 license, this
one forbids using the materials or their outputs to improve any other large language model unless it
is Meta Llama 3 or a derivative of it. Above 700 million monthly active users on the release date you
must ask Meta for a license, and all use must follow the Acceptable Use Policy.
