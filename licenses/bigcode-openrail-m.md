## Plain-English

The BigCode OpenRAIL-M agreement is a contract that bundles a copyright license, a patent license and
conditions on use. It permits free access, reuse and redistribution of the model and its modifications
for research, commercial or non-commercial purposes, as long as the use restrictions in Attachment A
always apply, including to modified material. When you share the model or a modification you must
carry those restrictions forward, either as written or as a similar set in your own contract, and
document your changes. Restricted uses include breaking the law, generating malware, deceiving by
impersonation and passing off machine-generated content without saying so. Because of those
restrictions BigCode itself says it is not an open-source license by the Open Source Initiative
definition.
