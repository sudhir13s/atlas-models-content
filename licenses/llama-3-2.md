## Plain-English
Llama 3.2 may be used, copied, modified and redistributed worldwide at no charge, including
commercially. When you distribute the model or a product that contains it, you give the recipient a
copy of the agreement, show "Built with Llama", and keep Meta's attribution notice. A model you train
or improve with the materials or their outputs must have a name that starts with "Llama". If the
products you or your affiliates offer had more than 700 million monthly active users on the release
date, you must ask Meta for a license first. Use must follow Meta's Acceptable Use Policy. The
materials are provided as is, and suing Meta over intellectual property in them ends your license.
