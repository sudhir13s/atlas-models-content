## Plain-English

Llama 2 may be used, copied, modified and redistributed worldwide at no charge, including
commercially. When you hand the materials or a derivative work to someone else you include the
agreement and keep Meta's attribution notice. This license has no "Built with Llama" display rule and
no model-name prefix rule, but it does forbid using the materials or their outputs to improve any
other large language model unless it is Llama 2 or a derivative of it. If your products had more than
700 million monthly active users on the release date you need a separate license from Meta, and all
use must follow the Acceptable Use Policy.
