## Plain-English

You can use, modify, distribute, and fine-tune Llama 3.1 commercially, including training on its
outputs (derivatives must carry a "Llama" name prefix and "Built with Llama" attribution). It is
**not** a standard open-source license: companies with more than 700 million monthly active users
must obtain a separate license from Meta, and all use is bound by Meta's Acceptable Use Policy.
Treat it as "open weight, commercially friendly, with conditions" rather than fully permissive.
