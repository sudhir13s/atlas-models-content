## Plain-English

You can use, modify, distribute, and fine-tune Llama 3.1 commercially, including training on its
outputs (a model built this way and distributed must carry a "Llama" name prefix, and distribution
needs "Built with Llama" attribution). It is **not** a standard open-source license: companies
whose products had more than 700 million monthly active users on the release date must obtain a
separate license from Meta, and all use is bound by Meta's Acceptable Use Policy.
Treat it as "open weight, commercially friendly, with conditions" rather than fully permissive.
