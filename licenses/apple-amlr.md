non-exclusive, revocable and limited to Research Purposes, which means non-commercial scientific
research and academic development; commercial exploitation, product development and use in a

redistribute and fine-tune the model, but every derivative must be labelled as one with its changes
disclosed, redistribution must include the agreement and Apple's attribution notice, and derivatives

breach it.
