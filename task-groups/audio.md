## Overview

Start here when the **input or the output is sound**.

- **Speech to text and text to speech** are the two most used tasks in this group.
- **Understanding audio:** label a sound, or ask a question about a recording.
- **Changing audio:** remove noise, separate speakers, convert one voice to another.
