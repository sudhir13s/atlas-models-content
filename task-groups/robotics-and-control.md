## Overview
Start here when the **output is an action** in a physical or simulated environment.

- **Robot policies** map camera images and instructions to motor commands.
- **Reinforcement learning agents** learn by trial and reward inside one environment.
- **Policies rarely transfer** to a different robot or environment without further training.
