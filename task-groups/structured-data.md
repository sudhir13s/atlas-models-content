## Overview

Start here when the data is a **table of rows and columns** or a **sequence of numbers over time**.

- **Tabular:** predict a category or a number for each row.
- **Time series:** predict the next values of a measured quantity.
- **Classical methods stay strong here**; gradient-boosted trees are the baseline to beat on tables.
