## Overview

Start here when the **output is new media**, usually steered by a text prompt or a reference image.

- **From nothing but a prompt:** image, video, 3D and audio generation.
- **From existing media:** editing, upscaling, restyling and animating a still image.
- **Check the licence first**; many of the strongest media models forbid commercial use.
