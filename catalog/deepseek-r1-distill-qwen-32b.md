summary: 'DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B is a language model released by DeepSeek-AI. According to its card it is Qwen2.5-32B fine-tuned on 800k samples curated with DeepSeek-R1, DeepSeek''s larger reasoning model. The card calls this distillation: the reasoning patterns of a larger model are transferred into a smaller dense model. It is one of six distilled checkpoints the card lists, from 1.5B to 70B parameters.'
intended_use: The card offers the distilled checkpoints to the research community, so that people can run and study smaller reasoning models, and it says the series supports commercial use and further distillation to train other language models. It asks readers to review its usage recommendations before running the models locally.
what_sets_it_apart: DeepSeek reports that reasoning patterns of a larger model can be distilled into smaller models, and says this gives better results than the reasoning patterns found by applying reinforcement learning directly to small models. The card also says the team slightly changed the configs and tokenizers of the distilled models, so their settings should be used.
running_it: The card recommends a temperature between 0.5 and 0.7 (0.6 preferred) to avoid endless repetition, and advises against a system prompt, with all instructions placed in the user prompt. For maths problems it suggests asking the model to reason step by step and put the final answer in a box. It also says the models sometimes skip their thinking step, and recommends starting every response with a "<think>" tag to force reasoning. It shows serving with vLLM or SGLang.
