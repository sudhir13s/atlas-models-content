## Summary

DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B is a dense language model distilled from DeepSeek-R1 using samples generated by the larger model. It is fine-tuned based on open-source models and uses reasoning data to achieve strong performance.

## Why It Matters

The model demonstrates that the reasoning patterns of larger models can be successfully distilled into smaller models, resulting in better performance compared to reasoning patterns discovered through reinforcement learning on small models.
