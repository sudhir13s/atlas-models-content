## Summary

DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B is a small language model from DeepSeek that writes out its reasoning step by step before answering. According to the model card it is built on Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B, a model from the Qwen team, and was fine-tuned by DeepSeek on samples generated by its larger reasoning model DeepSeek-R1. It is the smallest of six distilled models, a technique where a small model learns from the outputs of a bigger one.

## What Sets It Apart

DeepSeek states that the reasoning patterns of its larger model can be distilled into smaller models, and that this gave better results than the reasoning patterns a small model discovers through reinforcement learning on its own. The card adds that the distilled models come with slightly changed configs and tokenizers, so DeepSeek's own settings should be used.

## Running It

The card gives these settings for the R1 series: a temperature between 0.5 and 0.7 (0.6 recommended), no system prompt with all instructions placed in the user prompt, and for maths questions a request to reason step by step and put the final answer in \boxed{}. It also recommends making the response start with "<think>\n", because the models sometimes skip their thinking phase.
