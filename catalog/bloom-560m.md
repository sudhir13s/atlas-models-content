## Summary

Bloom-560m is a transformer-based multilingual language model developed by BigScience, designed to enable public research on large language models and serve as a pretrained base model.

## Intended Use

It is intended for text generation, exploring the characteristics of language generated by a model, and downstream tasks such as information extraction, question answering, and summarization.

## What Sets It Apart

Unlike standard GPT2 architectures, its technical specifications include a modified Megatron-LM architecture featuring layer normalization applied to the word embeddings layer (StableEmbedding) and ALiBI positional encodings with GeLU activation functions.

## Limitations

The model is considered out of scope for high-stakes settings, critical decisions, or material consequences on an individual's livelihood, and its outputs can include incorrect factual information, stereotypes, and hateful language.
