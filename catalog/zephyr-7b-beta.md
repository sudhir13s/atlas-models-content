## Summary

Zephyr-7B-beta is a chat model from the Hugging Face H4 team, the second model in the Zephyr series of assistants. The card says it is a fine-tuned version of Mistral-7B-v0.1, trained on a mix of publicly available synthetic datasets using Direct Preference Optimization, and that it is mainly English.

## Intended Use

The card says the model can be used for chat, and points to a demo to test it.

## What Sets It Apart

The card describes a two-step recipe: fine-tuning on a filtered version of the UltraChat dataset of synthetic dialogues generated by ChatGPT, then alignment with Direct Preference Optimization on UltraFeedback, 64k prompts with completions ranked by GPT-4. It says removing the built-in alignment of these datasets raised its score on MT-Bench, a chat-quality test.

## Limitations

The card says the model has not been aligned to human preferences for safety in a reinforcement-learning step, and is not run with response filtering as ChatGPT is, so it can produce problematic output, especially when prompted to. It also says the size and makeup of the base model's training data are unknown.
