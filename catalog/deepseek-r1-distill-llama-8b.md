summary: DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B is a reasoning-focused language model from DeepSeek. It is a smaller model that DeepSeek fine-tuned on samples generated by its larger DeepSeek-R1 model, a process called distillation, and the card lists Llama-3.1-8B as the model it starts from. The card says the distilled models can be used in the same manner as Qwen or Llama models.
intended_use: The card says the distilled models were open-sourced to support the research community, and that the DeepSeek-R1 series supports commercial use and derivative works, including distillation for training other language models. It adds that this model derives from Llama3.1-8B-Base and keeps that model's original llama3.1 license.
what_sets_it_apart: The card argues that the reasoning patterns of a larger model can be distilled into a smaller one, and says this gives better results than the reasoning patterns a small model finds through reinforcement learning on its own. Here the reasoning data written by DeepSeek-R1 was used to fine-tune an existing Llama checkpoint, rather than training a new model with reinforcement learning.
running_it: 'For the R1 series, the card recommends a temperature between 0.5 and 0.7, with 0.6 suggested, to avoid endless repetition or incoherent output. It also advises against a system prompt: all instructions go in the user prompt. Its own evaluations set the maximum generation length to 32,768 tokens.'
